【AI That Feels Human! How LLMs (Large Language Models) Create Natural Conversations】
Have you heard the term "LLM" lately?

It refers to AI that responds with natural, human-like language when you talk to it.
 For example, if you say, "It's cold today," it might reply, "Yes, you'd better put on some warm clothes."

Behind this ability to engage in natural conversations lies advanced technology that understands the intent and context of human language.

So, how exactly do LLMs achieve this natural conversation? Let's break down the mechanism into three steps!

① Learning from Massive Text Data:
LLMs learn the statistical relationships between words by training on vast amounts of text data from the internet (books, articles, conversations, etc.).

② Breaking Down Sentences to Understand Context:
Sentences are broken down into small units called "tokens" (words and characters), and the relationships between these tokens are analyzed. A technology called "Transformer" plays a crucial role in enabling appropriate interpretation based on context.

③ Generating Sentences Based on Predictions:
Using the learned information and contextual understanding, LLMs predict the words that should follow and generate natural sentences.

In essence, LLMs are "AI that predicts natural sentences like humans."
