MH PU Summit & Procurement Award 2025

Na primeira semana de Abril-25, estive reunido com os principais executivos, VPs e CPO de Procurement da MANN+HUMMEL, em Bad Herrenalb na Alemanha, para a realização do MH PU Summit & Procurement Award 2025.
Foi uma oportunidade ímpar de reencontrar nossos colegas de todos os continentes e de abordar temas como os impactos geopolíticos nos negócios globais, o roadmap dos objetivos de Procurement para materiais diretos e indiretos no FY25, além das estratégias necessárias para 2030, que foram amplamente debatidas e alinhadas.

Tive ainda a oportunidade de representar, por mais um ano, a nossa região LATAM no Procurement Award 2024/2025.
Nossa região foi reconhecida como destaque na categoria Sustainable & Resilient Supply Chain no FY2024.
Agradeço a toda a equipe LATAM de Procurement, Qualidade de Fornecedores e Logística, que atua todos os dias na construção do nosso Painel de Fornecedores.

Parabenizamos á toda equipe de Procurement de nossa Divisão na Alemanha, pelo profissionalismo na organização do evento.
Agradeço pela oportunidade ao nosso VP de Procurement, John Back, ao nosso VP da LATAM, Bert Kempeneers, e ao nosso CPO, Samuel Cochon.

‘’We are not immune to success’’
 One team, One Go, One Voice !

In the first week of April 2025, I joined the main executives, VPs, and CPO of Procurement at MANN+HUMMEL in Bad Herrenalb, Germany, for the MH PU Summit & Procurement Award 2025.
It was a unique opportunity to reconnect with our colleagues from all continents and to address topics such as the geopolitical impacts on global business, the roadmap for Procurement objectives for direct and indirect materials in FY25, as well as the strategies required for 2030 — all of which were thoroughly discussed and aligned.
I also had the opportunity to once again represent our LATAM region at the Procurement Award 2024/2025.
 Our region was recognized as a standout in the Sustainable & Resilient Supply Chain category for FY2024.
I would like to thank the entire LATAM team from Procurement, Supplier Quality, and Logistics, who work every day to build and strengthen our Supplier Panel.
We also extend our congratulations to the entire Procurement team from our Division in Germany for their professionalism in organizing the event.
I would like to thank our VP of Procurement, John Back, our LATAM VP, Bert Kempeneers, and our CPO, Samuel Cochon, for the opportunity.
‘’We are not immune to success’’
 One team, One Go, One Voice !
